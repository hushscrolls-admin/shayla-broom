The Magic Cupboard

For Shayla · Nana and the magic cupboard

1: Nana lives far away, but tonight she called with a secret. "Shayla," she whispered, "don't tell Poppy. I've found a magic cupboard."

2: "A magic cupboard?" I said. Billy sat on my lap and listened very carefully.

3: Nana showed me her cupboard on the phone. It was little and wooden. "Wiggle your fingers," she said, "and put something inside. It will come to me."

4: I looked at my bed. Right in the bed head there was a little door. Perfect. I opened it and wiggled my fingers. They felt fizzy, like lemonade.

5: "Okay, Nana," I whispered. "Ready?" I put in one jelly snake. Just one. "Don't tell Poppy," I said. "He always eats the red ones."

6: Nana opened her cupboard. "A jelly snake!" she gasped. Then she giggled. "Oh no. Poppy loves the red ones."

7: Nana put in a biscuit. I opened my bed-head door. There it was! Warm and crumbly. "Don't tell Poppy," Nana said. "He'll want half."

8: I put in a drawing of me and Nana and Billy. Nana put in a little note that said *Love you, Shayla.* I hugged the note. Billy tried to hug it too.

9: Then I had a big idea. I put in THREE jelly snakes. Red, green and yellow. "These are secret snakes," I told Nana. "Only for us."

10: Nana put in three more. And a chocolate frog. The cupboard was getting very full of secrets.

11: Suddenly we heard footsteps. "Shayla?" called Poppy, somewhere near Nana. "What's so funny?"

12: Nana's eyes went wide. "Quick!" she whispered. "Hide the snakes!" But the cupboard door wouldn't shut. The chocolate frog stuck out.

13: Poppy looked at Nana. Nana looked at me on the phone. I looked at Billy. Billy looked very guilty for a duck.

14: "Nana," I said softly, "maybe we can share? Just a little?" Nana smiled. "Just a little," she said.

15: We opened both cupboards. Nana gave Poppy a red jelly snake. I put one in for Dad too. Poppy said it was the best magic he'd ever tasted.

16: Nana winked. "Next time, Shayla, we'll send him the green ones." Poppy pretended not to hear. Billy gave a tiny happy quack.

17: "Love you, Nana," I said. "Love you, Shayla," she said. And the little door in my bed head stayed open… just a crack… for sharing.
