Shayla and the
Very Full Broom

A Halloween bedtime story

On Halloween night, when the moon was round, little witch Shayla flew high off the ground. She had a black hat, a starry dress and a grin, and a broom that went whoosh! with a swish and a spin.

Then whoosh! went the wind, and her hat blew away. A fluffy grey owl caught it. “Hoo! Can I stay?” “Hop on!” Shayla laughed, and the owl settled down. They zoomed over rooftops all over town.

Then whoosh! went the wind, and her wand tumbled free. A little brown puppy dug it out from under a tree. “Can I come too?” “Of course!” Shayla cried. So puppy climbed on, with his ears flapping wide.

Then whoosh! went the wind, and her bag of treats fell. A tiny green frog brought it back. “I can help as well!” “There’s room!” said Shayla. The broom gave a creak, and it wobbled and bobbled… and then, with a squeak,

SNAP! went the broom, and they all tumbled down into a pumpkin patch at the edge of the town. Out of the dark came a rumble and roar: a grumpy old troll who was hungry for more.

“A witch for my supper!” he growled with a glare. But owl gave a HOOT and flapped up in the air, puppy went WOOF, and frog went RIBBIT so loud that the troll ran away from that brave little crowd.

“You saved me!” said Shayla. “Now I’ll do my part.” She stirred up a spell with her wand and her heart. A pumpkin, a feather, a sparkle of light, and a new broom appeared, big enough and just right,

with a perch for the owl and a seat for the pup and a lily-pad spot for the frog to sit up. They flew home together beneath the bright moon, singing their Halloween witchy-broom tune.

And when it was bedtime, all cosy and warm, Shayla snuggled her friends, safe from wind and from storm. “The very best broom,” Shayla whispered, “it’s true, is one that’s so full there’s a friend there for you.”
